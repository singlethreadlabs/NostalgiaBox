# NostalgiaBox Bumper Video-Generation Prompts

These prompts create original station-identification bumpers for the proposed
NostalgiaBox lineup. They evoke late-1990s and early-2000s children's
television without reproducing an existing network logo, character, mascot,
song, slogan, or exact bumper.

## Shared production settings

- Duration: 6 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 4:3
- Resolution: 1440 × 1080
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Keep text inside title-safe margins.
- Hold the finished channel logo for the final second.
- Generate clean stereo sound with no copyrighted music.
- Export as MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio when possible.
- If a model renders text poorly, generate the visual without text and add the
  channel name afterward in a video editor.

## Channel 2 — Slime Time Rewind

**Programming identity:** Classic 1990s irreverent cartoons.

```text
Create a 6-second original children's television station-identification bumper
with the messy, tactile energy of late-1990s cable TV. Begin on a dark purple
background covered in faint VHS tracking lines. A blob of bright green slime
drops from above, splashes across the screen, and reveals chunky hand-cut
letters spelling “SLIME TIME REWIND.” Orange, turquoise, and purple geometric
shapes bounce around the logo with slightly imperfect stop-motion timing. Add
paper textures, photocopied outlines, fisheye distortion, analog color bleed,
and a quick burst of television static. The mood is mischievous, surprising,
and funny rather than gross or frightening. Include a rubbery splat, a short
scratching-record sound, and an energetic young announcer saying, “You’re
watching Slime Time Rewind!” Hold the completed logo for the final second.
Format: 4:3, 1440×1080, 30 fps. Use entirely original branding, lettering,
audio, and animation. Do not include existing network logos, characters,
mascots, slogans, or music.
```

## Optional transition variants

Once a station ID works, create matching variants by retaining its visual
identity and replacing only the spoken line and final card:

- “You’re watching **[channel name]**.”
- “Now back to the show.”
- “Coming up next on **[channel name]**.”
- “Stay tuned.”
- “Saturday mornings live here.”

Keep “coming up next” variants generic unless the show title will be added later
in an editor. Video generators frequently misspell specific program names.

## Shared negative prompt

Append this when the generator accepts a separate negative prompt:

```text
No existing corporate logos, network marks, copyrighted characters, celebrity
likenesses, recognizable theme songs, watermarks, illegible text, misspelled
channel names, extra words, distorted letters, flashing strobe effects, horror
imagery, realistic weapons, unsafe behavior, modern smartphones, contemporary
social-media interfaces, 16:9 framing, or content outside title-safe margins.
```
